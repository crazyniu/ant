# Asset provenance and placement

All runtime resources use project-relative `res://` paths. `docs/asset-manifest.json` inventories the included assets with SHA-256, byte size and image dimensions. Keep import settings: no mipmaps and alpha-border correction for sprites; original PCM import for WAV cues.

## Current runtime art

- `assets/serpent/environment.png`: generated warm-sand arena, independent of gameplay objects.
- `assets/serpent/serpent-atlas.png` and sibling JSON: original AI-generated eight-view head/tail and spherical body atlas, 1303 × 1774 RGBA. Use actual yaw regions, uniform physical scale and attachment anchors, never whole-card rotation or equal-grid slicing. `reports/assets/serpent-actor-assets.json` records source hashes, crop/alpha checks and provenance.
- `assets/serpent/serpent-armor-atlas.png`: independent intact/cracked gold overlays. Preserve the transparent center; recolor only the underlying body bead. Provenance and alpha checks are in `reports/assets/serpent-actor-assets-armor.json`.
- `assets/serpent/nest-entry.png` and JSON: a low open ground hole with its spawn anchor at the hole center, not the image bottom. Generation and measured anchor evidence are in `reports/assets/serpent-actor-assets-nest.json`.
- `assets/motion/ant-directions.png` and JSON: original eight-yaw ant views with measured foot/leg anchors and common 420-source-pixel physical width. Directional shader animation preserves fixed upper-left light; no reference-video pixels are copied.
- `assets/ants-atlas.png`, `assets/characters-atlas.png`, `assets/ui/fusion/` and `assets/ui/v03/`: active shared ant/actor, bead, audio-icon, panel and title atlases. Region JSON files retain alpha-derived bounds. Some slices are unused, but the atlas files are runtime dependencies and must not be removed.
- `assets/startup/title.png`: original generated ANT RESCUE brand, one aspect-preserving transparent element, not a complete-screen overlay.
- `assets/environment.png` remains a direct shared `main.gd` gallery dependency; current play uses the separate serpent environment. The gallery is not the acceptance target.

Live board objects, ant paths and effects share `scripts/board_plane.gd`; rules retain logical grid cells. AtlasTexture crops use filter clipping. Maintain sprite contact anchors, source alpha, aspect-preserving titles and uniformly scaled nine-slice corners.

## Font and audio

`assets/fonts/Title.ttf` is DejaVu Sans Condensed Bold. Its third-party license is preserved verbatim in `licenses/DejaVu.txt`.

The audio pack contains one full 40-second original Ogg Vorbis score and 14 original PCM WAV cues generated by the bundled algorithmic source. The service preloads all cue paths, including disabled-tool cues, so all remain included. No third-party recordings, samples or borrowed melodies are included. See `assets/audio/README.md` and `manifest.json`.

## Rights and boundary

AI image generation and original audio provenance are not legal clearance warranties or a new license grant. Applicable owner and service terms still apply; no project-wide open-source license has been invented. Source-generation filenames are retained only as provenance identifiers, never private absolute paths or download URLs. Generated working files, videos, screenshots, caches, archives, credentials, internal conversations, unused old levels and unverified map/shop artwork are excluded.
